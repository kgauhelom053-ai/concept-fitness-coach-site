05 — FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Still unsure? Book the intro call — it’s free and there’s no pitch deck.

Do I need a full gym?

No. Programs are written for your equipment — full gym, home setup, or a mix. We’ll map what’s available on the intro call.

How much time per week?

Most clients train 3–4 days for 45–70 minutes. We’ll design around the time you actually have, not an ideal week.

Is nutrition included?

Build and Peak include habit-based nutrition guidance and targets. Foundation focuses on training first; nutrition add-ons are available.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Coaching is month-to-month after the first 4-week block. No long contracts. Give 7 days’ notice before your next billing date.

Who is this not for?

If you want a quick fix, extreme cuts, or zero accountability, this isn’t it. Ridge Fit is for people ready to train consistently and communicate weekly.
